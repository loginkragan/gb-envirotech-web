Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding micropropagation, supply stages, and transport.

What is the advantage of tissue-cultured plantlets over conventional suckers or cuttings?

Tissue cultured plants are clonally multiplied under sterile, disease-screened conditions. They provide 100% true-to-type genetic uniformity, synchronized flowering and harvesting, vigorous vegetative growth, and significantly higher survival in the field.

Can I order plants at the in-vitro stage or rooted ex-vitro stage?

Yes. GB ENVIROTECH supplies plants at all 4 stages (in-vitro sealed vessels, rooted ex-vitro, primary hardened in polyhouses, and field-ready secondary hardened). Nurseries with their own polyhouses frequently order at primary or rooted stages to save freight costs.

How are plantlets packaged for transit across India?

In-vitro vessels are dispatched in shock-proof, temperature-buffered cartons. Primary hardened plants are shipped in specialized nursery crate racks, while secondary plants in polybags are dispatched via dedicated agricultural freight.

How does custom protocol research work?

Clients provide explants or mother material under a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). We systematically calibrate sterilization, shoot multiplication, rooting, and hardening parameters, culminating in a validated Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).
