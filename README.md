# GB ENVIROTECH Corporate Website (gbenvirotech.com)

Modern corporate website for **GB ENVIROTECH**, a plant biotechnology and commercial plant tissue culture enterprise operating out of RIICO Industrial Area, Boranada, Jodhpur, Rajasthan, India.

## Features Included in This Build

1. **Brand Identity & Leadership Presentation:**
   - Trade Name: **G.B. ENVIROTECH**
   - Proprietor: **Gursharan Singh Bains**
   - Operations Leadership / CEO: **Dr. Gurdeep Bains**
   - GST Record: `08AHDPB6648K1Z6`
   - Strict adherence to factual claims governance.

2. **Interactive 4-Stage Supply Model:**
   - Stage 01: In-Vitro Culture Vessels
   - Stage 02: Rooted Plantlets (Ex-Vitro transfer ready)
   - Stage 03: Primary Hardened (Polyhouse acclimatised)
   - Stage 04: Secondary Hardened (Field-ready saplings)

3. **Filterable 7-Crop Portfolio:**
   - Grand Naine (G9) Banana (*Musa acuminata*)
   - Pomegranate (*Punica granatum*)
   - Kagzi Lemon (*Citrus aurantifolia*)
   - Tissue Cultured Bamboo (*Bambusa balcooa / B. tulda*)
   - Commercial Orchids (*Dendrobium & Phalaenopsis*)
   - High-Yield Moringa (*Moringa oleifera*)
   - Saffron Micropropagation (*Crocus sativus*)

4. **Facility & Infrastructure Tour:**
   - 4,000 sq. ft. Cleanroom Laboratory (Micro-filtered positive air, Laminar Airflow consoles, Culture rooms)
   - 1,200 sq. ft. Controlled Polyhouse Hardening Unit (Pad & fan evaporative cooling, high-pressure foggers)
   - 4,000 sq. ft. Auxiliary space
   - Solar-powered operations + Reverse Osmosis (RO) water purification plant

5. **Custom Protocol Optimisation & Contract R&D:**
   - 5-step collaborative R&D workflow under bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

6. **Commercial Lead Capture:**
   - Interactive B2B bulk quotation form
   - Custom R&D protocol enquiry tab
   - Direct WhatsApp message generation
   - Mobile-responsive navigation and accessible design
